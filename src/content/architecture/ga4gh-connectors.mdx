### Key behaviors
- Resolves dataset URIs and intervals to concrete URLs/streams.
- Enforces site auth (LS-AAI/GA4GH AAI) and policies before download.
- Shared across Agent and Remote WES/TES executions.

### Practical tips
- Preconfigure allowed endpoints and auth scopes per site.
- Cache reference genomes (refget) locally in agents to reduce egress.
- Log connector access for lineage and compliance.
